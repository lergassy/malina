Frequently asked questions

Can I apply for a US visa while living in Bali?

Yes. A US visa is filed where it suits you to attend the interview, not by citizenship. Indonesia accepts applications, and living here is not strictly required, though residence helps with appointment slots.

Where does the interview take place?

At the embassy in Jakarta or the consulate in Surabaya. There is no US consulate on Bali, only occasional outreach sessions, so plan for the flight.

How much is the consular fee?

USD 185 at the time of writing, with an increase to USD 435 under discussion. The fee is paid before booking and is not refunded on refusal. Our commission is billed separately.

Is a KITAS required?

Not formally — you can book without one. But residents get priority on slots, and a residence permit strengthens the application by showing ties to where you live.

Can the interview be done online?

No. Attendance in person is mandatory.

What language is the interview in?

English. An interpreter is possible, but their admission is agreed in advance rather than settled on the day.
