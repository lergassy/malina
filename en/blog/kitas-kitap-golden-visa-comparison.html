Short Answer

Four main paths to long-term stay differ in entry threshold and rights:

  • KITAS — work permit for expats with an employer, family, or business. 1-2 years, renewable.
  • KITAP — permanent status after several years on KITAS. 5 years, opens path to citizenship.
  • Golden Visa — premium program for investors from $350k or talents. 5-10 years.
  • Second Home Visa — most accessible path ($130k deposit) for those not planning to work in Indonesia.

Golden Visa: Two Subtypes

Golden Visa is not one visa, but a program with two paths:

TypeCodeRequirementsDuration
InvestorE28C$350k for 5 years / $700k for 10 years in government bonds, stocks, or deposit. Or $1M real estate5-10 years
TalentE33BDegree from top-100 global university with GPA 3.5+5-10 years
Important: investments must be maintained for the entire visa duration. Withdrawal = visa cancellation. 183+ days/year = Indonesian tax residency.

Second Home Visa: Key Points

  • Deposit of IDR 2 billion (~$130k) in a state bank (BNI, BRI, Mandiri) for the entire visa duration
  • Or real estate worth $1 million
  • Work in Indonesia prohibited, but remote work for foreign employer — yes
  • Does not provide direct path to KITAP or citizenship
  • 5 or 10 years without annual renewals
Gray area: some sources claim Second Home can be converted to KITAP. No official confirmation — check with immigration.

Path to Citizenship

The only confirmed path to Indonesian citizenship:

  • KITAS (any type) → 2-5 years continuous stay
  • KITAP (permanent permit) → 5 years continuous OR 10 years with breaks
  • Naturalization: language proficiency, cultural understanding, clean record, renouncing previous citizenship

Golden Visa and Second Home Visa are not direct paths to citizenship — you must first transition to KITAP.
