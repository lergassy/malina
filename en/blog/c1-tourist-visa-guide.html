Bali C1 visa 2026: half a year, no exit

The C1 is a single-entry tourist visa, index 211A. It gives 60 days from the date of entry and extends twice by 60 days each — up to 180 consecutive days without a single border run. Arranged online before departure, all-in from IDR 2,500,000. The main limitation: leaving the country cancels it.

Updated: 25 August 2026 · Verified by the Malina Visa team

How the C1 works

The C1 is a single-entry tourist visa, index 211A. It gives 60 days from the date of entry and extends twice inside the country by 60 days each — up to 180 consecutive days. It is the standard choice for a long winter stay: half a year on the island without a single border run.

  • ✓60 days on entry, then two 60-day extensions — a ceiling of 180 days
  • ✓Arranged online before departure, from any country, with no consulate visit
  • ✓The autogate works with an electronic C1 on arrival
  • ✓A sponsor is required — for our clients, we act as the sponsor
  • !The visa is single-entry: leaving Indonesia cancels it, and you cannot return on it
  • !Flying in and out? You need a D1 or D2 multiple-entry visa, not a C1

Timing and extensions: 60 → 120 → 180

Extensions are filed at the immigration office covering your address. The official fee for each 60-day extension is IDR 2,000,000, processed within 3 working days of payment.

Days 1–60

The first period starts on the date of entry. An extension may be filed no earlier than 14 days before it ends.

First extension

Another 60 days. The new period starts the day after the old one — no days are lost.

Second extension

The final 60 days. No further extension is possible: 180 days is the legal ceiling.

After that

Either you leave, or you change status to a KITAS — and that has to start early, not in the last week.

File with at least 6 working days to spare. Miss the deadline and it is an overstay: IDR 1,000,000 per day, per person. With our support the dates are our problem: we remind you and file early.

C1 or e-VOA: when the extra cost pays off

The price difference is real, so the number to compare is the cost of the whole trip, not of the visa.

  • ✓Staying up to 60 days — take the e-VOA at IDR 900,000; a C1 is money wasted
  • ✓Staying 3–6 months — the C1 is cheaper and calmer than a chain of border runs and fresh e-VOAs
  • ✓Thinking of staying — the C1 converts to a KITAS without leaving the country, and that is the decisive argument

A detailed comparison of cost and timing is in C1 or e-VOA.

Documents and cost

The list is short, but the passport requirements are strict — that is where most applications fail.

  • ✓A passport valid for at least 6 months on the date of entry
  • ✓A clean scan of the photo page — no glare, no cropped corners
  • ✓A photo on a white background
  • ✓A return or onward ticket and your address in Indonesia

The all-in price is from IDR 2,500,000, and processing takes a few working days. Extensions are paid separately at the official rate. Full terms are on the C1 visa page.

What you cannot do on a C1

The C1 is a visit visa. It carries no right to work or to earn inside the country, and that is not a technicality: according to immigration, working on a tourist visa is one of the leading causes of deportation from Bali.

  • !Working for Indonesian companies or earning income inside the country
  • !Providing services to local clients, commercial filming and advertising work
  • !Long-form study and working as a journalist

If you work remotely for foreign clients there is a dedicated status, the E33G. If you run a business in Indonesia, look at the investor KITAS.

Frequently asked questions

How long can I stay in Bali on a C1 visa?

Up to 180 consecutive days: 60 days on entry plus two extensions of 60 days each. That is the legal ceiling — there is no third extension.

Can I leave and come back on a C1?

No. The C1 is single-entry: leaving Indonesia cancels it and you cannot return on it. If you plan trips in and out, you need a D1 or D2 multiple-entry visa.

How much does a C1 extension cost?

The official fee is IDR 2,000,000 for each 60-day extension; turnkey with us it is IDR 2,500,000, or IDR 3,500,000 rushed. There are at most two. Processing takes up to 3 working days after payment; filing opens 14 days before the current period ends.

Can I convert a C1 into a KITAS without flying out?

Yes — changing status from a visit permit to a limited stay permit is provided for, and it is one of the C1's main advantages. The procedure is not quick, so start it early rather than in your final weeks.

Do I need a sponsor for a C1 visa?

Yes, the visa requires a sponsor. For our clients we act as the sponsor, so there is no one for you to find.
