How Much Capital Do You Need for Investor KITAS

Personal Share10 Billion IDR~600,000 USD
PT PMA Capital2.5 Billion IDR~150,000 USD
For KITAP15 Billion IDR~900,000 USD

The Main Mistake: Confusing Company Capital and Personal Share

Since October 2025, the minimum paid-up capital for PT PMA has been reduced to 2.5 billion IDR (approximately 150,000 USD). This is a requirement for the company itself, fulfilled during registration.

However, for investor KITAS E28A there is a separate requirement: the personal share of a specific shareholder must be at least 10 billion IDR (approximately 600,000 USD). This is confirmed by the official FAQ on evisa.imigrasi.go.id.

A company can be registered with 2.5 billion capital, but a specific shareholder will not qualify for KITAS if their share is less than 10 billion.

How the Share Is Calculated

  • The share is calculated based on the Deed of Establishment and shareholder register
  • Must be registered directly in the applicant's name — not through a nominee
  • Applicant must be both shareholder AND director or commissioner
  • Being only a shareholder without a position — does not qualify

Documents Confirming Capital

  • Company bank account statement showing amount, date, and PT PMA name
  • Surat Pernyataan Penyetoran Modal — notarized capital contribution statement
  • SWIFT confirmation of transfer (for foreign transfers)
Important: contributed capital is locked for 12 months — cannot be withdrawn for non-commercial purposes.

Common Reasons for Rejection

  • ✕Mismatch between OSS data and incorporation documents
  • ✕Overdue or missing LKPM reports
  • ✕Shareholder without director or commissioner position
  • ✕"Paper" capital without bank statements
