Key Point: LKPM Is Quarterly, Not Annual

Many people think PT PMA files one big annual report. In reality, LKPM is submitted every quarter — it's an investment activity report filed through the OSS system (oss.go.id).

The annual report is the tax return (SPT Tahunan), filed separately to the tax office (DJP) by April 30.

Important: the report is only considered filed when the status in OSS becomes "Disetujui" (approved), not just "Terkirim" (sent).

What LKPM Includes

  • Quarterly investments: capital, equipment, renovations
  • Working capital: salaries, rent, utilities
  • Personnel: total count, breakdown of Indonesians and foreigners
  • Production: revenue, volume of services provided
  • Changes: director/shareholder/address changes
  • Issues: obstacles during the period (if any)

Penalties for Missing LKPM

There's no direct monetary fine, but administrative penalties escalate:

  • 1First miss — written warning (surat peringatan)
  • 2Repeated misses — business license suspension
  • 3Extreme case — NIB revocation

How Missing LKPM Affects Investor KITAS

There's no direct legal link, but a chain reaction occurs:

Missing LKPM → NIB Freeze → Company can't sponsor visas → KITAS can't be renewed

If the NIB is frozen, the PT PMA cannot act as a visa sponsor. This affects all company-sponsored KITAS types: investor E28A, work, and family.

In practice: immigration checks the sponsor company's status during KITAS renewal. NIB problems are grounds for rejection.

Annual Tax Return: SPT Tahunan

This is a separate report filed to the tax office (DJP) by April 30 — for companies with a calendar fiscal year.

Tax must be paid before filing the return. Late filing penalty: IDR 1,000,000 (~$63).

How to File LKPM Through OSS

  • Log into OSS RBA (oss.go.id) with your NIB account
  • Go to the LKPM section
  • Fill in data for the quarter
  • Submit the report
  • Wait for "Disetujui" status — only then is the report counted
