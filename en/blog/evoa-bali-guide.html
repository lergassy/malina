Bali e-VOA 2026: your visa before you fly

The e-VOA is an electronic visa on arrival arranged online before departure. It gives 30 days with one 30-day extension — 60 days maximum per entry. The government fee is currently IDR 500,000; our all-in price is IDR 900,000. Its main advantage over a visa bought at the airport: no payment queue, and the autogate works.

Updated: 25 August 2026 · Verified by the Malina Visa team

What the e-VOA is and who it suits

The e-VOA is a visa on arrival arranged in advance through the government portal. On paper it is the same B-series visa issued at the airport, but it reaches you by email before departure, and at the border you show nothing but your passport.

  • ✓Tourism and holidays — the main scenario
  • ✓Visiting family and friends
  • ✓Business meetings, negotiations, conferences and exhibitions
  • ✓Transit through Indonesia with a stop in the city
  • !You cannot work on an e-VOA — not employed, not freelancing for local clients
  • !Studying and journalism are not covered by this visa either
  • !Selling services and running day-to-day operations belongs to business visas

e-VOA versus a visa at the airport

The status is formally identical. The difference is what happens after the plane lands.

  • ✓No payment queue. The visa is already tied to your passport; you go straight to passport control
  • ✓The autogate works. With a biometric passport, clearance takes 15–25 seconds
  • ✓Extending is simpler. Visa, passport and entry record are linked electronically from the start
  • ✓Refusals surface early. A document problem shows up at home, not at the border after a 12-hour flight
The price gap is about to widen. On 12 August 2026 immigration announced plans to raise the fee: IDR 750,000 for a visa arranged online before departure and IDR 1,000,000 for one obtained at the airport. Both currently cost IDR 500,000. No effective date has been set, but the direction is clear: arranging in advance will save you a quarter of a million rupiah, not just some queueing.

Timing: 30 plus 30, and not a day more

The e-VOA gives you 30 calendar days from the date of entry. It can be extended exactly once, by exactly 30 days — 60 days maximum per entry. There is no second extension.

The extension fee is IDR 500,000. You may file no earlier than 14 days before your current permit ends and no later than the day it ends. Processing takes up to 3 working days after payment, and the new period starts the day after the old one, so no days are lost.

  • !Need more than 60 consecutive days? The e-VOA will not do — take a C1 visa from the start
  • !An overstay costs IDR 1,000,000 per day, per person
  • !Leaving and returning for a fresh e-VOA is technically possible, but frequent visa runs draw attention at the border

What you need to apply

The list is short, which is part of why this visa is so popular:

  • ✓A passport valid for at least 6 months on the date of entry
  • ✓A scan of the passport photo page
  • ✓A photo — an ordinary one, no studio requirements
  • ✓A return or onward ticket
  • ✓Your address in Indonesia

We arrange the e-VOA all-in for IDR 900,000: we check the documents before filing, submit, pay and send the finished visa to your inbox. That pre-check is not a formality — the most common causes of refusal are a name spelled differently from the passport and a passport with under six months left.

The autogate: crossing the border in 20 seconds

The automated gates on arrival in Denpasar and Jakarta work with a biometric passport and an approved electronic visa. The system reads the chip and matches your face — immigration reports clearance in 15–25 seconds.

  • ✓You need a chipped passport — look for the symbol on the cover
  • ✓Children aged 6 and over may use it, subject to the usual requirements
  • ✓With a child under 6, one parent goes through a staffed counter
  • ✓The All Indonesia declaration is filed online before landing
Separately from the visa, Bali's tourist levy of IDR 150,000 is paid through the Love Bali portal. Details in our piece on the Love Bali levy.

What if 60 days is not enough

Two routes, and you choose before you fly — changing your mind inside the country is harder and costs more.

  • ✓The C1 visa — 60 days on entry, extendable in 60-day steps up to six months. From IDR 2,500,000. Compared with the e-VOA in C1 or e-VOA
  • ✓D1 and D2 multiple-entry visas — a year of entries, up to 60 days each. From IDR 5,000,000
  • ✓E33G — if you work remotely for foreign clients: a year without extensions

One important difference between the e-VOA and visa-free entry: from an e-VOA you can change status to a limited stay permit. With visa-free entry that is not possible at all — worth knowing for citizens of Kazakhstan and Belarus, who since July 2026 need no visa for tourism.

Frequently asked questions

How does the e-VOA differ from a visa at the airport?

The status is the same — a B-series visa for 30 days. The process differs: the e-VOA is arranged online before departure, so you skip the payment queue, can use the autogate, and learn about any document problem in advance rather than at the border.

How many times can an e-VOA be extended?

Once, by 30 days. The maximum per entry is 60 days. The extension fee is IDR 500,000, filed no earlier than 14 days before expiry and no later than the day it expires.

How much does an e-VOA cost?

The government fee is currently IDR 500,000. Our all-in price is IDR 900,000, which covers document checking, filing, payment and the finished visa in your inbox. Immigration has announced plans to raise the fee to IDR 750,000 for online applications; no date has been set.

Can I work on an e-VOA?

No. The visa covers tourism, visiting family and business meetings, but not work — neither employment nor freelancing. For remote work with foreign clients there is the E33G; for employment in Indonesia, a work KITAS.

Can I convert an e-VOA into a KITAS without leaving?

Yes, changing status from a visit permit to a limited stay permit is provided for. That sets the e-VOA apart from visa-free entry, where a change of status is prohibited outright. The procedure is not quick, so plan ahead.
