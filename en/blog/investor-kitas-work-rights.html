Investor KITAS and Work: What PT PMA Owners Can Do

DirectorFull ManagementNo IMTA or RPTKA
CommissionerOversight OnlyOperations forbidden
PenaltyUp to IDR 500M+ deportation

Short Answer

A director with investor KITAS E28A can fully manage their company: sign contracts, hire employees, negotiate deals, and represent the company. A separate work permit (IMTA) is not required.

A commissioner with E28A is limited to oversight functions. If a commissioner wants to work operationally, they need a work KITAS E23 plus IMTA.

The main restriction for everyone: you cannot work for another employer, freelance, or earn income from activities outside your PT PMA.

What a Director with E28A Can Do

  • Sign contracts on behalf of the company
  • Hire and fire employees
  • Manage day-to-day operations
  • Conduct meetings and make decisions
  • Represent the company in commercial relationships
  • Monitor financial performance
  • Open and manage bank accounts
Key advantage of E28A: A director-investor with a share of at least IDR 10 billion is exempt from IMTA (Foreign Worker Employment Permit) and RPTKA (Foreign Workforce Utilization Plan). This is the main difference from work KITAS E23.

What Is Forbidden

  • ✕Working for another employer
  • ✕Freelancing or remote work for other companies
  • ✕Earning income from activities outside your PT PMA
  • ✕Activities outside the company's registered KBLI codes

For commissioners, operational work is additionally forbidden: if you want to do more than oversee, you need a director position or a separate work KITAS.

Gray Area: Director's Salary

No regulation explicitly states whether a director with E28A can receive a salary. The regulatory logic:

  • E28A is for investors who earn through dividends
  • E23 is for employees who receive salaries

In practice: if immigration sees regular salary payments to a director-investor, they may treat this as "employment" and require a work KITAS E23. It's safer to distribute income through dividends.
