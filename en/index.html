How Bali visas work: the short version

Indonesia splits visas into three tiers. The first is short trips: eVOA gives you 30 days plus one 30-day extension. The second is long visits without residency: the C1 tourist visa and C2 business visa stretch to 180 days through extensions, while multiple-entry D1/D2 let you come and go for one to two years. The third is residency: KITAS E33G for people employed by companies abroad, investor and family KITAS, and after several years KITAP, permanent residence.

Three rules that save money: a visa cannot be swapped for another without leaving the country (a few KITAS routes are the exception, and they run through a bridging visa), every extension since June 2025 requires biometrics in person at an immigration office, and overstaying costs IDR 1,000,000 per day per person. How each type is extended is covered on our visa extension page; what to do if you have already overstayed is in the overstay guide.

Planning to work for yourself rather than just live here — that is what PT PMA is for, a company with 100% foreign ownership. It also opens the way to an investor KITAS and a local bank account. We publish breakdowns of every status, prices and rule changes in the blog, each article stamped with the date its facts were checked.
