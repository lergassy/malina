Frequently asked

Where do I start if I do not know which service I need?

Start with the visa finder: four questions and you see the right type, term and price. If your case is unusual, message us on WhatsApp or Telegram and we will sort it out in one conversation.

Are the prices on this page final?

They are turnkey prices: the government fee is already inside unless stated otherwise. Separately payable levies such as DKP-TKA are always named. The full table lives on the price page.

Why do business services say "on request"?

The cost of setting up and running a company depends on the activity, the ownership structure and the state your paperwork is already in. We send the calculation within an hour of a short call.

Do we need to sign a contract?

No. The relationship is governed by our public offer, which is accepted by payment — there is no separate document to sign.
